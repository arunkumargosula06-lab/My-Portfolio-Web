# Case Study 02 — Morrow & Crumb

A fictional neighbourhood bakery website concept created by Aruu Everlance to demonstrate small-business website design.

- Responsive single-page layout
- Accessible navigation and clear section hierarchy
- Sample menu cards, story section and enquiry call-to-action
- No real business, client, testimonials, address, opening hours, sales claims or product prices
- Contact CTA routes to the Aruu Everlance business email

Live route after deployment: https://aruu-everlance-webin-omega.vercel.app/case-study-02/

This is a self-initiated portfolio demo, not paid client work.
